# Announcing: the post, social previews, reels

Read this when the graphic goes out with a chat post, as a social preview, or as a short video.

## Posting order

- Get the graphic approved before it is posted. Most chat tools cannot swap the image on a sent post, and a saved draft cannot hold an image, so each revision after posting means deleting the post and posting again.
- If you can only save a text draft, say plainly that the person attaches the image themselves.
- One image per post is the common limit. Merge before and after into one picture (stacked for wide screens) rather than sending two.

## The post text

- Brief and casual, in the poster's voice ("I", "we"), about what the reader can now do: "You can un-archive a chat again", not "Un-archive support has shipped".
- Replace internal terms with plain words. If a score or metric appears, say in one clause what it measures.
- Links (the PR, the long version, the docs) go in the text, not in the image.
- A follow-up post is titled as improvements since the launch, drops anything a teammate already announced, and can credit each change's author.

## Social preview (1200 by 630)

- The headline and the product, nothing else. No bullets, no chart.
- Judge it at about 500 px wide, the size a link card shows. Try two or three compositions side by side at that size and keep the one whose headline and picture still read.

## Reels

- About 30 s, horizontal. One beat of about 3 s per change, ordered by how much a person feels each one.
- Real-speed clips that cut in. No slow motion, no sliding or zooming clips, no animated transitions.
- A loop is truly periodic: the last frame leads into the first, and every change on screen is caused by something visible (a click, a keystroke). A colour that drifts on its own reads as broken.
- Same rules as stills for before and after: same input on both sides, staged so the difference shows within the clip.
